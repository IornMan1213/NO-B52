B-52J Stratofortress for Nuclear Option, version {VERSION}
https://github.com/IornMan1213/NO-B52

INSTALL
1. You need BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter {BLUEPRINTER} or newer.
2. Delete any older B-52J Stratofortress_*.nobp (or the old B-52J_Stratofortress folder) from BepInEx/plugins
   first, or the game loads both.
3. Make a folder Nuclear Option/BepInEx/plugins/B52J/ and put everything from this zip in it.
   It holds the aircraft (.nobp) and B52Systems.dll; the game needs both.
4. Strongly recommended: Heavy Aircraft Hangar (https://github.com/IornMan1213/NO-HeavyHangar).
   The B-52 is too big for stock hangars. Without it, the B-52 spawns on the apron outside a hangar.
In multiplayer, every player needs the same version of this mod.

FLYING IT
  Speedbrakes      Throttle to idle.
  Drag chute       On the runway at idle, hold the wheel brakes below 165 kt.
                   It drops off below 20 kt or when you add power.
  Crosswind crab   [ and ] turn the main trucks 5 degrees left or right (up to 20), \ straightens them.
                   Keys can be changed in BepInEx/config/com.ironman1213.b52systems.cfg.
  Taxi steering    Rudder.
  Takeoff          At full load it lifts off by itself at about 170 kt. Climb at 180 kt.
  Approach         150-165 kt depending on weight. Flaps below 204 kt. Touch down at 135-145 kt.

SETTINGS (BepInEx/config/com.ironman1213.b52systems.cfg)
  Telemetry.Enabled   Writes a CSV log of your flights to BepInEx/B52_telemetry (off by default).
  Gear.Crab*          The crosswind crab keys.
